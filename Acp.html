My Morning Routine

A great school day starts with a calm, energizing, and organized morning:

  1. Wake up at 6:00 AM and do light stretching.
  2. Brush teeth and freshen up.
  3. Enjoy a healthy traditional breakfast.
  4. Pack the school bag and check the timetable.
  5. Head out to school with a positive mindset by 7:30 AM!
